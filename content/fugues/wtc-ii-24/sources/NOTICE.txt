J. S. Bach: Fugue in B minor, BWV 893. WTC II, no. 24.

Score: Kroll, Bach-Gesellschaft XIV (1866), pp. 200-202.
Humdrum encoding: David Huron; revised by Craig Sapp.
https://github.com/humdrum-tools/bach-wtc-fugues/blob/master/kern/wtc2f24.krn
Original transcription retained as bwv893.original.krn. The closing
appoggiaturas C-sharp–B and E–D-sharp are restored from Kroll,
realised as quavers followed by crotchets, as permitted by Tovey.

Analysis checked against Hermann Keller, Das Wohltemperierte Klavier
(1965), pp. 186-187, linked at the author’s website; not reproduced.

Donald Francis Tovey, The Well-Tempered Clavier II (1924), pp. 174-175.
Complete original pages retained; source links included in each extract.
