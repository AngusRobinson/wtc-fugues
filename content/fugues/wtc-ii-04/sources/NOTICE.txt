The underlying composition and Kroll's 1866 edition are public domain.
David Huron and Craig Stuart Sapp are credited for the digital transcription.
The original Humdrum file is preserved without alteration. The derived MEI
separates all three voices and makes sounding accidentals explicit, including
tied notes across bar-lines. Both engraved versions preserve every note and tie.
