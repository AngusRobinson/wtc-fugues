# Fugue in B-flat minor, BWV 867

Book I, no. 22. Tonal answer, recurring crotchet figures, paired stretti and the final five-part stretto.

The study includes an interactive annotated score, two- and 5-stave PDFs, and analyses by Keller, Prout and Tovey. Source and edition details are in [sources/NOTICE.txt](sources/NOTICE.txt).
