Bach, Fugue in A minor, BWV 865. WTC I, no. 20.

Score: Kroll, Bach-Gesellschaft XIV (1866), encoded by David Huron; revised by Craig Sapp.
https://github.com/humdrum-tools/bach-wtc-fugues/blob/master/kern/wtc1f20.krn

The downloaded source is retained unchanged. Its commented bars 80–87 are restored in the derived score. Malformed final tie syntax and a duplicated E unison in one part are corrected; the final A unison between distinct parts is retained. The ending has been checked against Tovey and the independent Mutopia edition. The long tonic pedal is retained; a pedal-board or an additional player may be needed.
https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/BachJS/BWV865/BWV865_Fuga20/BWV865_Fuga20-let.pdf

Analysis follows Hermann Keller, Das Wohltemperierte Klavier von Johann Sebastian Bach, pp. 101–103. The six formal groups follow Keller; the score has 87 bars, and the bar 73 pair is bass/alto (Keller prints 85 bars and bass/soprano).
https://www.hermann-keller.org/assets/downloads/547e2aeb/bwv865.pdf

Public-domain extracts: Ebenezer Prout, Analysis of J. S. Bach's Forty-Eight Fugues, ed. Louis B. Prout (1910), pp. 45–46; Donald Francis Tovey, The Well-Tempered Clavier I (1924), pp. 128–129. Full scan links are included in each extract.

Prout’s ‘A major’ heading for Fugue 20 is a misprint for A minor.
